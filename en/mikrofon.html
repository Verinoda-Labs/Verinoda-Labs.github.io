Microphone Bridge

Streams a phone or computer microphone live to a Bluetooth speaker.

Streams a phone or computer microphone live to a Bluetooth speaker, filters ambient noise, records and plays audio files.

Web Audio

Features

  • Live microphone to speaker

  • Ambient noise filtering

  • Recording and audio file playback

The microphone needs HTTPS, which GitHub Pages provides. Open it on the device connected to the speaker.
