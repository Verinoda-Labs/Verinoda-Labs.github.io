About

Verinoda Labs builds tools that make AI use efficient and cut its cost, and a few games and apps besides. One idea runs through the tools: spend fewer tokens, get more trustworthy results.

Our story

Verinoda began on 23 September 2026 under the working name RepoAtlas: an evidence-first code analysis derived from Graphify. The first releases came on 26 September; by 25 October it reached 0.5.0.

We saw that coding agents can speak about code with confidence and be wrong. Our answer was to turn every answer into a claim that carries a file and a line number, and to say “unknown” when there is no evidence.

Verinoda is open source (Apache-2.0) and in beta. It is derived from Graphify and is not an official Graphify release.

Our principles

  1. 01

    Evidence comes first

    Every claim arrives with a file and a line range. The narrowest range is shown: the lines that actually carry the statement.

  2. 02

    Status is part of the claim

    A claim can be verified, an inference or unknown. We never present “maybe” as “certain”.

  3. 03

    “I don't know” is an answer

    Without evidence we do not guess. The answer is “unknown”, with the next step beside it.

  4. 04

    A graph is a lead, not verification

    A link between two symbols is only a lead. To count as verified, the source lines have to be read.

  5. 05

    The decision is yours

    The tool does not recommend a choice; it gives what the code says about the decision and the questions a person should answer.

  6. 06

    Criticism is a hypothesis

    An objection to an answer is recorded as a hypothesis to look for evidence about.

  7. 07

    Performance, with its measurement

    Claims are published only with their measurement, including the ones that did not go our way.
