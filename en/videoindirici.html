Desktop app

Video Downloader and Format Converter

A four-tab desktop app: it downloads video and audio, converts file formats, repairs unfinished recordings and brings a microphone voice forward.

Version 1.4.0

A four-tab desktop app that can be packaged as a double-click EXE. Downloading has quality choice, playlists, embedded subtitles, range trimming, a batch queue and automatic channel watching through Windows Task Scheduler. Converting covers video, audio, image, document and e-book formats, plus compression to a target size. ffmpeg arrives with the app through the imageio-ffmpeg package.

Python (yt-dlp, ffmpeg); can be packaged as an EXE; Windows Task Scheduler for auto-watch

Features

  • Download Video: Downloads video or audio (MP4 or MP3) from Twitter (X), YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo and more than 1000 other sites. It has quality choice (best, 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p, 480p, smallest file), playlists, Turkish and English subtitle embedding, taking only a chosen range, and a batch queue.

  • Convert Format: Converts video, audio, image, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF, TXT, CSV, JSON, HTML, Markdown and e-book (epub) files to other formats. It has ready presets (WhatsApp, Instagram square, Reels/TikTok/Shorts, e-mail attachment) and two-pass compression to a target size. PDF from Word, PowerPoint and Excel needs that program installed on the computer.

  • Repair Video: Repairs unfinished or damaged recordings (a power cut during OBS, a crash, a pulled cable). It writes an analysis report first, then tries methods in order, from quick repair to recovery from the raw stream. The original file is not touched. Files whose table of contents (moov) was never written need an intact reference recording made with the same OBS settings; only the picture is recovered, the sound does not come back.

  • Balance Audio: Brings the voice of the person on the microphone forward when game, music or the other side's audio drowns it. It measures every audio track, guesses the microphone track and plays a 15-second before/after sample. The picture is not re-encoded; only the audio is processed.

  • Adobe compatibility: When ticked (the default), H.264 is preferred for the picture and the sound is converted to AAC, so the downloaded MP4s open with sound in Premiere and After Effects.

Facts

  • Version 1.4.0; a four-tab desktop app
  • With the microphone on its own audio track, the speech-to-background gap went from 2 dB to 25 dB in a test
  • When everything is mixed onto one track the voices cannot be fully separated; the measured gain is about 4 dB
  • Target-size compression example: a 54 MB, 1-minute video became 15 MB (target 16 MB)
  • Automatic watching works on Windows only
  • Only public content can be downloaded; private posts or ones that need a login cannot

Only download content that is your own or that you have permission for; respect copyright and terms of use.
