01What does this site collect?

This site uses no cookies, no visitor tracking and no analytics tool. Fonts, scripts and images load from the site's own server together with the pages; no request goes to third parties.

02Contact form

The contact form does not send data to a server. When you press the button, the name, email address, topic and message you wrote open as a ready draft in your email app. You choose whether to send it.

If you send us an email, we use the information you wrote only to reply to you.

03External links

When you open the GitHub, PyPI and app links on this site, those services' own privacy terms apply.

04Data controller

Data controller: Verinoda Labs. The trade name, address and data-protection contact details will be added here before publication.
